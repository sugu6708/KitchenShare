    Instructions

    1

    Prep Ingredients

    Mince the garlic and roughly chop the fresh basil. Set aside a few basil leaves for garnish.

    2

    Boil Pasta

    Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil. Add the penne and cook according to package instructions (about 10-12 minutes) until al dente.

    3

    Make Sauce

    In a large skillet, heat olive oil over medium heat. Sauté the minced garlic until fragrant. Pour in the crushed tomatoes, season with salt and pepper, and let simmer for 8 minutes.

    4

    Combine and Serve

    Drain the pasta and add it directly to the skillet with the sauce. Toss well to coat. Stir in the chopped basil. Serve immediately, garnished with remaining basil.
